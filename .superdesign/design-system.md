# Gutto Rocha portfolio — design system

Calm, editorial, dark portfolio of a Head of Design. Black canvas, off-white ink, generous whitespace, big confident headings, photography and case imagery carry the color.

## Color
- surface #000000 (page), card #151d20, card-alt #172329, line #334047
- ink #f8f8f5, subtle #b8b8b1, muted #a3a39c, dim #92928a
- accent (buttons, active nav) #efefea with on-accent #171717
- each case has its own brand color (Creator Ads: magenta #b5176d-ish) used only for small accents like the play button
- no gradients except a dark shade over photos to keep text legible

## Type (DM Sans only)
- display clamp(42px,6.4vw,120px), weight 700, tracking -0.065em, leading 1.06
- section clamp(30px,4.2vw,76px), subheading clamp(26px,2.6vw,44px), card title clamp(22px,2.1vw,34px)
- body clamp(16px,1.15vw,20px) leading 1.6–1.85, caption 12–14px
- label/eyebrow 12px uppercase, tracking .06em, muted color

## Shape & space
- radii: tag 8px, note 16px, card 24px, media canvas clamp(24px,2.5vw,40px), controls/pills 999px
- page gutter 10vw each side; reading width 65ch
- sections separated by large vertical rhythm (120–200px on desktop)

## Components
- Floating pill top nav (dark translucent, logo at left, links, white "Português" pill)
- Pill buttons: primary off-white on black, secondary outlined with #666660
- Case meta strip: label (muted, small) over value (ink, ~32px bold) in columns
- Rules: no colored accent bars in front of text; helper text in plain muted color
